Problems and logs

The app keeps a light log of what it read and found (titles, not your typing), sent to your server and deleted after 24 hours. Report a problem right when it happens; on the TV, hold ≡ for 3 seconds.

About and credits

Marquee Lens keeps your film lists on this device. Nothing is stored on a server.

This product uses the TMDB API but is not endorsed or certified by TMDB. Film details and IMDb ratings come from OMDb. Where-to-watch data is by JustWatch, via TMDB. Recognition by Google Gemini.

See IMDb ratings on the posters in Netflix, yes, HOT and other apps. Tap a rating for the same film details as camera mode.
